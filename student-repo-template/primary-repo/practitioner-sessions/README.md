# Practitioner Sessions

There are two separate graded things here, and they are commonly confused.

## 1. Participation, 100 points

**One paragraph per session, posted to that session's Canvas discussion.** Not to this repo.

Each paragraph names two things:
- one thing you learned that is not in the course readings
- one question you would ask the practitioner if you had more time

Nine sessions are scheduled and **at least eight paragraphs are required**.

**You do not have to attend live.** Every session is recorded and posted. Watching the recording
earns the same credit as being on the call. Post by the Sunday following the session.

## 2. Practitioner Engagement, 100 points

**Three longer analyses, chosen from across the nine sessions.** Due at the end of the semester.

Use `engagement-01.md`, `engagement-02.md`, `engagement-03.md` in this directory. Each is one to
two pages, six sections, and the grade turns on the section asking where the practitioner
confirms, challenges, or extends the course material.

You do not have to decide now which three. Take notes as you go and develop the three that gave
you the most to argue with.

## If you already made session-NN-writeup.md files

Keep them. They are good working notes and several of you have already filled some in. They are
not themselves graded. When you pick your three sessions, move that content into the matching
`engagement-NN.md` file.

The template previously shipped eight per-session files, which implied a write-up was required
every week. That was wrong and it has been corrected here. Nothing you have written is wasted.
