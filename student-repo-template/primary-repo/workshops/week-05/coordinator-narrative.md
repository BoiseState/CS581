# Coordinator Narrative — Workshop 5

One page. The scenario gives you three frameworks with three clocks, three recipients, and
three definitions of done. Write what a single incident coordinator would need to manage all
three at once.

Answer, from the incident you reconstructed, not in the abstract:

- What is the one timeline that serves all three notifications, and who had to contribute to
  it before you could build it?
- Where do the three clocks start from different moments, and how did you resolve which
  moment is real for each?
- What could you not determine from your own system, and whose evidence filled the gap?
- What does no framework resolve: the question that is still open after every required
  notification is made?
